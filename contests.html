    Contest questions

    Does it cost anything to enter?

    No. Both contests are free, and no purchase or donation ever improves your chances.

    Who can enter?

    People aged 16 and older (with guardian consent where needed) in the countries listed in the official rules. Employees of Next.Education and sponsors, and their households, can't enter.

    How is the prize paid?

    By bank transfer or another method we agree with the winner, after eligibility checks. Winners in Canada must correctly answer a skill-testing question.

    Can I use AI to write my essay?

    No. Spelling and grammar checks are fine, but the ideas and writing must be yours. Judges may ask finalists follow-up questions.
