About the match

How does the match work?

Your answers are compared against our program-path library and guides to build a starting plan: the route, the program types worth shortlisting, the tools to run your numbers and the funding sources to check. If you opt in, we can also introduce you to accredited providers that fit your answers.

Will schools call me?

Only if you tick the partner-consent box on the final step. Without it, you'll only receive your report and any emails you asked for.

Is the advising call a sales call?

No. Advisors help you think through options. If you'd like introductions to schools, you can ask for them; nobody will push you.

Can I use this if I'm outside North America?

Yes. The match covers learners anywhere, with extra detail for the US, Canada, the UK, India and Australia.
