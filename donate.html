Keep it free for the next student

Every tool, guide and match on Next.Education is free. Reader support pays for the research, new tools, student prizes, outreach and the people behind it, and keeps us independent of any school.

Choose your gift

Pledge your gift

Tell us your gift and we'll email a secure payment link (card, PayPal or bank transfer) within one business day. Nothing is charged until you complete that link.

Next.Education is an independent publisher, not a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible. Gifts support our operations and are non-refundable except in cases of error. Questions? Contact us.

Donation questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

No. Next.Education is an independent publisher, not a registered charity, so gifts aren't tax-deductible in any country.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Yes, any time. Reply to any receipt or contact us and we'll stop it before the next charge.

Can my company sponsor instead?

Yes. Sponsorships come with recognition on the contest or tool you fund. See advertise and sponsor.

Does donating affect what you publish?

No. Donors and sponsors never influence our editorial content or rankings. See our editorial standards.
