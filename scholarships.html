Scholarship questions

What does "full funding" mean?

Usually tuition plus a living allowance, and sometimes travel and visa costs. Each funder defines it differently, so read the official terms for what's covered.

When should I apply?

Many major international awards close 6 to 12 months before the program starts, and some open a year ahead. Start researching at least 12 to 18 months before you plan to begin.

Can I apply for several scholarships at once?

Usually yes, though some awards can't be combined. Applying widely, especially to smaller and local awards, is one of the most reliable ways to win something.
