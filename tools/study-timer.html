Settings

A long break follows every fourth focus session. A short tone plays at the end of each block. Keep this tab open; the countdown also shows in the tab title.

How to make each session count

  1. Set one target. Write the single thing you'll finish this block, for example "solve problems 4 to 9" or "explain chapter 3 from memory".
  2. Start with recall. Spend the first five minutes writing what you remember from last time before looking at notes. That retrieval practice is one of the best-supported study techniques.
  3. Work, don't reread. Do problems, make flashcards or teach the idea out loud. Rereading feels productive but does less.
  4. Break properly. Stand up, move, get water. Skip your phone if you can; it makes restarting harder.
  5. Space it out. Return to the same topic over several days instead of cramming it into one.
