How the quiz scores you

Each answer adds points to one or more of six routes: degree, certificate, bootcamp, apprenticeship, study abroad and free-first. Your goal and your time horizon carry the most weight, because they decide which credentials can get you there. Budget and learning style then separate close calls.

The result is a starting point, not a verdict. If your top two routes are close, compare them on real numbers with the program comparer and the ROI calculator.

The six routes in one line each

  • Degree: the gate for licensed professions and research careers, and the widest set of options.
  • Certificate: one job-ready skill set in months, often stackable into a larger qualification.
  • Bootcamp: an intensive switch into tech roles, worth it only with credible outcomes.
  • Apprenticeship: paid on-the-job training toward a recognised trade.
  • Study abroad: a qualification plus an international network, with visas and costs to plan.
  • Free-first: test an interest with open courses before you spend money.
