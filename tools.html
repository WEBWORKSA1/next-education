Free tools for education decisions

Seven free tools that turn a big, fuzzy decision into numbers you can compare. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing you type leaves your device.

Which route fits me?

Seven questions that point you to a degree, certificate, bootcamp, apprenticeship or free-first path.

Education ROI calculator

Payback period and 10-year net gain for any program, including the income you give up while studying.

Student loan calculator

Monthly payment, total interest and a payoff schedule for any loan amount and rate.

Student budget planner

Monthly costs vs income for home or abroad, with a gap you can plan scholarships around.

GPA calculator

Weighted 4.0-scale GPA from letter grades or percentages, with a target-grade helper.

Program comparer

Put up to three programs side by side on cost, time, format and outcomes.

Focus study timer

Pomodoro-style sessions with a session log, built for retrieval practice.

Which tool should I start with?

  • No idea which route? Start with the route quiz.
  • Choosing between programs? Use the program comparer, then the ROI calculator on your top pick.
  • Worried about money? The budget planner shows your gap; the loan calculator shows what borrowing really costs.
  • Applying soon? Check where you stand with the GPA calculator.
  • Already studying? The focus timer keeps sessions short and sharp.
