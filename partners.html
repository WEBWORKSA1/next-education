For schools and training providers

Next.Education matches people to programs by field, level, format, country, budget and start date. Partner schools receive consented enquiries from people who fit their programs, nothing else.

How partnership works

  1. Tell us your programs. Fields, levels, formats, intakes, countries you recruit from and your entry requirements.
  2. We match and filter. Only people whose answers fit your criteria, and who gave explicit consent to hear from schools, are passed on.
  3. You receive enquiries. Delivered in real time by email or to your CRM, with the answers each person gave so your team can follow up well.
