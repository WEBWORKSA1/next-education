How to learn from free resources without stalling

Free courses have one big weakness: nobody makes you finish. These habits fix most of that.

  1. Pick one course, not five. Choose a single course in your top field and commit to four weeks.
  2. Schedule it like a class. Put two or three fixed sessions a week in your calendar and use the focus timer.
  3. Produce something. Finish each week with an output: a problem set, a short project or a summary you could teach from.
  4. Decide at the end. After four weeks, ask whether you want more of this. If yes, that's a strong signal to invest in a credential. Get matched when you're ready.

Free resources can also lead to real credit and credentials. See free and low-cost ways to earn credentials.
