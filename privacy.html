Privacy policy

What we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. In short: we collect only what you send us or what's needed to run the site and its ads, and we never sell your personal information.

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Who we are

Next.Education ("we", "us") operates the website at next.education. For any privacy question or request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

2. Information you give us

When you use a form on this site (the program match, advising and consultation requests, newsletter and alert sign-ups, contest entries, donation pledges, job applications, partnership and contact forms), we collect the details you enter. Depending on the form, that can include your name, email, phone number, country, study level, field of interest, budget range, start window, essay text, portfolio links and your message. We also record which consent boxes you ticked, the page you used and basic campaign information from the link that brought you here (such as UTM tags), so we can keep an accurate consent record.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Browser storage. We store your theme, saved shortlist, cookie choice, quiz progress and similar preferences in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and isn't sent to us.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • Advertising cookies. See section 6.

4. How we use information

  • To deliver what you asked for: your match report, advising call, alerts, newsletter, contest entry or a reply to your message.
  • With your separate, explicit consent only: to introduce you to accredited schools or licensed education providers that match your request.
  • To run contests, verify winners and pay prizes.
  • To process donation pledges and send payment links.
  • To improve the site, prevent spam and abuse, and comply with law.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with education providers you have consented to hear from, limited to the details relevant to your request;
  • with service providers that help us run the site, including our form-delivery service (FormSubmit), email and payment providers, who may process data only on our instructions;
  • when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

6. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (Europe). Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

When you first visit, our cookie notice lets you choose "Accept all" or "Essential only". Choosing "Essential only" asks Google to show non-personalised ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may also see Google's consent message, which records their choices. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

7. Embedded content and third-party services

  • YouTube. Videos load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Once playing, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts. Our typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • External links. We link to official and third-party sites we don't control. Their privacy practices are their own.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected: match and enquiry data for up to 24 months, newsletter data until you unsubscribe, contest records for up to 3 years after the contest ends (to meet legal and audit needs), and job applications for up to 12 months. We then delete or anonymise it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time and to complain to a regulator. These rights come from laws including the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CPRA), Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Australia's Privacy Act. To exercise them, send a privacy request through our contact form. We'll respond within the time the law requires.

You can unsubscribe from any email using the link it contains. Withdrawing consent to hear from schools stops future sharing; please also tell any school that has already contacted you.

10. Children

Next.Education is intended for people aged 13 and older, and our contests for people aged 16 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Security and international transfers

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect information. No online service is perfectly secure. Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes

We'll update this policy when our practices change and show the new effective date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.
