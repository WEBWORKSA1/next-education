About Next.Education

We help people make one of the most expensive decisions of their lives with better information: which route to take, which program to pick and how to pay for it.

Why we exist

Choosing what to study is hard, and most of the information online is written to sell you something: a program, a loan or a consultation. Rankings rarely explain their methods, salary claims rarely cite a source and the true cost of a credential, including the income you give up while studying, is almost never shown.

Next.Education is our answer. We compare every route on the same terms, link to official sources instead of summarising rules that change and give you tools to run the numbers yourself. Then, if you want it, we'll match you with programs that fit.

What we do

  • Compare routes: degrees, certificates, bootcamps, apprenticeships, study abroad and free courses.
  • Build free tools: ROI, loan, budget and GPA calculators, a program comparer, a route quiz and a study timer.
  • Publish guides: on accreditation, scholarships, financial aid, studying abroad, learning and career change.
  • Fund students: through our essay prize and monthly award.
  • Match learners to programs: with consent, and only with accredited or recognised providers.

How we make money

Next.Education is free to use. We earn revenue from advertising (including Google AdSense), from schools and training providers that pay to feature programs or receive consented enquiries, from sponsorships and from reader donations. Paid placements are always labelled. Partners never pay for coverage in our guides and can't change our tools or recommendations.

Who we serve

High-school students and their parents, college students, adult learners and career changers, in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, India and Australia, and anywhere else people are deciding what to learn next.

Independence

Next.Education is an independent website. We are not a school, an admissions agent or a lender, and we're not affiliated with any company or institution that uses a similar name. See our disclaimer and trademark notice.
