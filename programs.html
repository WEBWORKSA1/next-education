How to read these cards

Time is the typical full-time length. Part-time and online versions often take longer; accelerated tracks can be shorter.

Cost is a band, not a price, because fees swing widely between countries, public and private schools, and in-state or international status. As a rough guide, $ is under about $5,000 in total, $$ is up to about $20,000, $$$ up to about $60,000 and $$$$ above that. Use the ROI calculator with a school's real fees.

Outlook marks fields where employers in many of the countries we cover consistently report hiring need. For current pay and demand, check official labour-market sources such as the US Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook, Canada's Job Bank or the UK National Careers Service.

The last line on each card is the gate you'll have to pass: a licence, a portfolio or an exam. Plan for it from day one.

Before you shortlist anything

  • Confirm the school is accredited or recognised by the right body. Here's how.
  • For licensed careers, check the program is approved by the licensing regulator where you want to work.
  • Ask for outcomes data: completion rates and graduate employment, with how they were measured.
  • Compare total cost, not tuition alone. Fees, materials and lost income add up.
  • Search for scholarships before you apply, not after you're admitted.

Program explorer questions

Are these real schools?

The explorer compares program types (paths), not individual schools, so you can decide on a route first. When you click "Find programs", we match you with accredited options for that path.

What does "Featured program slot" mean?

It's a paid placement that schools can buy to show their program to people browsing a field. Featured listings are always labelled and never change our editorial content.

Where is my shortlist saved?

In your browser only. We don't see it unless you send it to us through the match form.
