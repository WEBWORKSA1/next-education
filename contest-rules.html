Official contest rules

These rules govern the Next Step Essay Prize and the Next Step Monthly Award. No purchase or donation is necessary to enter or win, and none improves your chances.

Last updated: October 30, 2026. Prize amounts and dates for each season are shown on the contests page and form part of these rules.

1. Sponsor

The contests are run by Next.Education ("Sponsor", "we"). Questions about the contests can be sent through our contact page. These contests are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Google, YouTube or any other platform.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are 16 or older at the time of entry and legal residents of the United States (50 states and DC), Canada (excluding Quebec), the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand or India. Entrants under the age of majority in their place of residence must have permission from a parent or legal guardian, who may be asked to co-sign winner documents. Employees, contractors and judges of the Sponsor and of any prize sponsor, and members of their immediate households, are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Entry periods

  • Essay Prize: each season runs between the opening and closing dates and times shown on the contests page (Eastern Time). Entries received after the close are not judged.
  • Monthly Award: each calendar month is a separate entry period, from 12:00:00 a.m. on the first day to 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the last day.

4. How to enter

Essay Prize: submit one original essay of 150 to 600 words in English responding to the season's prompt, using the entry form on the contests page. Monthly Award: complete the entry form on the contests page. Entries must include accurate contact information.

5. Limits

One essay per person per season, and one Monthly Award form entry per person per month. Use of multiple identities, email addresses, automated tools or any other method to exceed these limits voids all of that person's entries.

6. Bonus entries and free alternative method

For the Monthly Award, each eligible entrant receives one base entry and may receive bonus entries for using free features of the site, as shown on the contests page (for example, completing the route quiz). Alternatively, any entrant may receive one bonus entry per month, without using any feature, by sending a request through our contact form with the topic "Contest" and the words "Free bonus entry" plus their full name and the email used to enter. No purchase, donation or subscription is required for any entry, and no payment increases the number of entries.

7. Judging and selection

Essay Prize (skill-based): eligible essays are screened for compliance and then scored by at least two judges against four equally weighted criteria: clarity of purpose (25%), specific detail and evidence (25%), originality of voice (25%), and reflection and realism (25%). Ties are broken by the highest score for originality of voice. Judges' decisions are final. Essays generated substantially by artificial intelligence, plagiarised or submitted elsewhere for publication are disqualified.

Monthly Award (random selection): one potential winner is selected by random draw from all eligible entries (including bonus entries) received in the month, within ten days after the month ends. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries received.

8. Skill-testing question

Before being confirmed as a winner of either contest, a potential winner resident in Canada must correctly answer, without assistance, a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question administered by phone, video call or email.

9. Prizes

Prize amounts are stated on the contests page in US dollars and may be paid in the winner's local currency at a reasonable exchange rate on the payment date. Prizes are paid by bank transfer or another method agreed with the winner, within 30 days of confirmation. Prizes are not transferable. The Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a stated prize becomes unavailable. If a season receives fewer than 20 eligible essays, the Sponsor may extend the entry period or award only some prizes.

10. Winner notification and verification

Potential winners are notified by email. They must reply within 10 days and complete a declaration of eligibility and release (and, for minors, a parent or guardian co-signature). If a potential winner can't be reached, doesn't respond in time, is found ineligible or doesn't comply with these rules, the prize may be forfeited and an alternate winner selected.

11. Taxes

Winners are responsible for any taxes, fees or reporting obligations on their prize under the laws of their country. Where the law requires, the Sponsor may request tax information before paying a prize.

12. Publicity and entries

Except where prohibited, winners agree that their first name, last initial, country, level of study and, for essay winners, their essay or an excerpt may be published on Next.Education and in our newsletter and social channels, without further compensation. Entrants keep copyright in their essays and grant the Sponsor a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use winning and finalist essays for these purposes.

13. Privacy

Personal information collected for the contests is used to administer them and as described in our privacy policy. Opting in to newsletters or partner contact is separate and never required to enter.

14. General conditions

The Sponsor may cancel, suspend or modify a contest if fraud, technical failure or any other factor beyond its reasonable control impairs its integrity, and may then award prizes from eligible entries received before the action. The Sponsor isn't responsible for lost, late, incomplete or misdirected entries, or for technical failures of any kind.

15. Limitation of liability

By entering, entrants release the Sponsor, prize sponsors and their respective officers, employees and agents from liability for any loss or damage arising from participation in a contest or the acceptance or use of a prize, to the extent permitted by law.

16. Governing law and winners list

These rules are governed by the laws applicable where the Sponsor is established, without regard to conflict-of-law rules, and subject to any mandatory consumer protections in the entrant's place of residence. For a list of winners, see the contests page or request it through our contact form within 60 days of a contest's end.
