Study abroad questions

Do I need an education agent?

No. Many students apply directly through university websites and official portals. If you use an agent, check whether they're paid by universities (most are), ask which schools they represent, and never let them hold your passport or log-ins.

When should I start?

About 12 to 18 months before your intended start. Tests, scholarship deadlines and visa processing all take time, and the best funding closes early.

Which country is cheapest?

It depends on your citizenship, program and lifestyle. Some European countries charge low or no tuition at public universities for certain students, but living costs and language requirements vary. Use the budget planner with real figures from each university.

Can I work while studying?

Many countries allow limited part-time work for international students, but the rules differ and change. Check the official immigration site for your destination and never plan a budget that depends on breaking those limits.
