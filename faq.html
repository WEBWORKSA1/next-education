Frequently asked questions

The quick answers. Can't find yours? Contact us and we'll reply within two business days.

Using Next.Education

Is Next.Education free?

Yes. All tools, guides, the program match and advising calls are free. We're funded by advertising, labelled partner placements, sponsorships and donations.

Do I need an account?

No. Your shortlist and tool settings are saved in your own browser. We only receive information you choose to send through a form.

Which countries does the site cover?

Guides are written for learners in the US, Canada, the UK, India and Australia, and most advice applies anywhere. Where rules differ, we name the official body to check.

Are you a university or an admissions agent?

No. We're an independent publisher. We don't award credentials, make admission decisions or charge students fees.

Matching and privacy

What happens after I get matched?

You'll see your plan on screen and receive a report by email. If you ticked the partner-consent box, accredited schools that fit your answers may contact you. If you didn't, they won't.

Do you sell my personal information?

No. We share details with education providers only when you explicitly consent, and only those relevant to your request. See the privacy policy.

How do I unsubscribe or delete my data?

Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or send a privacy request through the contact form and we'll action it.

Scholarships and contests

Are the scholarships in your finder real?

Yes. We list long-running awards from governments, foundations and universities and link to the official site. Always confirm current eligibility and deadlines there.

How do I enter your contests?

On the contests page. Both the essay prize and monthly award are free to enter. Read the official rules.

Supporting and partnering

Are donations tax-deductible?

No. Next.Education isn't a registered charity. Donations support our operations, student prizes, hiring and outreach.

How can my school or company work with you?

See for schools and advertise and sponsor. For offers on the website or domain, use the contact link at the top of every page.
