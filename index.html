Questions people ask first

Is Next.Education really free?

Yes. Every tool, guide and the match itself are free. We're funded by advertising, by schools that pay to feature their programs (always labelled) and by reader donations.

Do you sell my information?

No. If you ask to be matched or to hear from schools, we share your details only with the education providers relevant to your request, and only with your consent. Read the privacy policy.

Which countries do you cover?

Our guides and tools are written for learners in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, India and Australia, and most advice applies anywhere. Where rules differ, we name the official body to check.

Are you a school or an admissions agent?

Neither. Next.Education is an independent publisher. We don't grant credentials or make admission decisions.
